Hi, I’m Pei-Chi.

I’m a PhD student at the University of Houston, advised by Emani Hicks in the AWARE Lab. I’m interested in human-centered AI for health and social wellbeing, both developing the models and designing how people interact with them through wearables and apps. My research interests include:

  • Human-centered AI for health and social wellbeing
  • Human–AI interaction with LLMs and ML systems
  • Community-centered design of intelligent technology

Current project

Study runs fall 2026 · 2 weeks per participant

EarMark

For people with misophonia, hyperacusis, or sensory sensitivity, some everyday sounds are overwhelming — but not every time. EarMark pairs short phone check-ins with Apple Watch data to understand what makes those moments different. No audio or location is ever stored.
